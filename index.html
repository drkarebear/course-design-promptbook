The recurring pattern

Explore first. Choose second. Build third. Check last.

The prompts are designed to keep you in control. AI can generate possibilities quickly; you decide what is worth developing.

The course-design workflow

A clear path from COR to course

Move through the steps in order for a new prep, or jump to the part you need. Each step has a specific job.

  1. 0📄

    Understand the Course

    Decode the COR and get a clear picture of what the course requires, permits, and leaves to faculty design.

    COR Decoder coming soon
  2. 1🧭

    Find the Units

    Explore 3 meaningfully different ways to organize the course into 3–5 major learning arcs before choosing one structure to develop.

    Open Find the Units
  3. 2🎯

    Design the Destinations

    Explore several culminating paper, project, performance, or demonstration ideas for each learning arc, then develop only the one you choose.

    Open Design the Destinations
  4. 3↩

    Work Backward

    Identify what students must learn, see modeled, practice, and receive feedback on before the culminating work.

    Open Work Backward
  5. 4🗓

    Map the Semester

    Turn the learning arcs into a realistic weekly plan with purposeful pacing, usable feedback windows, and manageable workload.

    Open Map the Semester
  6. 5📚

    Match the OER

    Choose readings and materials because they support the learning—not because they happen to be in the book.

    Open Match the OER
  7. 6🛠

    Build the Learning

    Create activities, practice, and smaller assignments that deliberately build toward the destinations.

    Open Build the Learning
  8. 7🔗

    Check the Build-Up

    Make sure each important assignment prepares students for something that comes later. Find the orphan tasks.

    Open Check the Build-Up
  9. 8✓

    Audit the Course

    Return to the course-specific rubric to check alignment, scaffolding, repetition, workload, and missed COR content.

    Open Audit the Course

About the project

Practical prompts for real faculty work

Course Design Promptbook is an independent faculty-created resource for California Community College educators. It is designed to make AI-supported course planning more intentional, transparent, and manageable.

It is not an official California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office tool and does not replace your college’s COR, curriculum process, accessibility review, or faculty judgment.
