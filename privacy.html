Privacy

Keep course design useful without bringing student data along.

Course Design Promptbook is a static faculty resource. You can browse the site and copy prompts without creating an account here or submitting information to this site.

Short version

What happens on this site?

  • No Course Design Promptbook account or sign-in is required.
  • The site does not include forms, advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts in this version.
  • Copy buttons work in your browser; they do not send your prompt to Course Design Promptbook.
  • If you paste a prompt into another AI service, that service's privacy terms apply.

On Course Design Promptbook

What this site collects

The current site does not ask you to provide your name, email address, course files, student information, or other personal information. It does not contain a Course Design Promptbook login, submission form, comment system, advertising network, or analytics script.

Like most hosted websites, the service that hosts the site may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver web pages, such as an IP address or browser request information. That processing is governed by the hosting provider rather than by Course Design Promptbook.

When you use the prompts

Your AI tool is a separate service.

Copying a prompt does not send it anywhere. Once you paste a prompt, COR, course plan, or other material into an AI platform, that platform's account settings, privacy policy, data controls, and institutional agreements apply.

Do not include student records or personally identifying student information.

Course design normally does not require student names, grades, identification numbers, disability information, individual accommodations, private communications, or other student records. Remove or generalize that information before using an external AI tool.

If your college provides an approved AI platform or specific guidance for faculty use, follow those local requirements.

Beyond this site

External links and tools

Course Design Promptbook may link to other websites or tools. Those destinations operate under their own privacy and accessibility practices. A link from this site does not mean that Course Design Promptbook controls how another service collects, stores, or uses information.

If the site changes

Privacy information will change with the features.

If a future version adds a form, analytics, an embedded third-party service, a login, or another feature that changes data handling, this page should be updated before that feature is treated as part of the regular site experience.
