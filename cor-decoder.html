Understand the Course with COR Decoder

Start with the official Course Outline of Record before deciding how to organize, teach, or assess the course.

The goal: separate what the COR requires from what it illustrates or leaves to faculty design, then turn that interpretation into useful planning tools.

What COR Decoder creates

Turn a dense COR into a course-design roadmap.

Upload or paste your COR, then choose the planning tool you need. You can create either document or both.

First-Time Faculty Course Guide

A concise, brain-friendly overview of what the course is really asking students to learn and do, including easy-to-miss requirements and areas of faculty choice.

Course-Specific Alignment Rubric

A detailed, course-specific rubric for examining whether an OER, developing course, or completed course meaningfully teaches the COR.

Have your official COR ready. You can upload the file or paste the COR text into COR Decoder. You do not need to do both.

After you decode the COR

Use the guide and rubric as your design anchors.

The Faculty Guide helps you see the shape of the course. The alignment rubric gives you a course-specific check you can bring back at the end. Neither one designs the course for you.

The COR sets the boundaries. You make the design decisions.

Once you understand the course, move into Step 1 and explore several possible unit structures before choosing one to develop.
